    - How would you design the data storage and retrieval system to handle the massive volume of real-time data?
    - What kind of message queues would you implement, and for what purpose?
    - How would you handle potential failures or disruptions in the system?
    - How would you monitor the performance and health of the platform in real-time?
    - How would you design the API's that the mobile applications would use?
    - How do you handle the potential for fraud?
